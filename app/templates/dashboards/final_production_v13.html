Percentage Calculation Logic

Base quantities

Inspection Qty (Prod)= Sum of qty where Tnature = PROD
Subtotal Rejp= Rejection + Rework + A + B + CA + B + C only for Seamless (U2). Welded (U1) = Rejection + Rework.
Total Inspection= Inspection Qty (Prod) + Subtotal RejpThis is the denominator for every % on the dashboard.

Percentage formulas

% Rejection= Rejection ÷ Total Inspection × 100
% Rework= Rework ÷ Total Inspection × 100
% A + B + C= (A + B + C) ÷ Total Inspection × 100U2 only.
% Total Rejp= Subtotal Rejp ÷ Total Inspection × 100
% of inspection(KPI)= KPI value ÷ Total Inspection × 100
% Share(Item Group)= Item Group Total Inspection ÷ Unit Total Inspection × 100

Rules

  • Item group cell % uses that plant + item group's own Total Inspection.
  • Plant Total & Grand Total % = sum of numerators ÷ sum of Total Inspection (weighted). Not an average of the cell %.
  • Measure follows header toggle — MT, No's or Meter. Same formula, different quantity, so % on No's basis can differ from MT basis.
  • Slicers apply first — % uses only filtered data.
  • Blank / 0 Total Inspection → % left blank (no divide by zero).
